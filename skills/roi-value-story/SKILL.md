# ROI value story

## Establish the value contract

1. Require verified account, usage, and renewal context; use operational, revenue, cost, and customer-note evidence only when supplied.
2. Separate realized value, observed adoption, scenario estimate, expected value, and missing evidence.
3. Read [references/value-scorecard.md](references/value-scorecard.md) before calculating a scenario or choosing value metrics.
4. Never infer realized ROI from monthly revenue, churn probability, adoption, or a model driver.

## Build the narrative

1. Connect a supported capability to a named workflow, measurable adoption signal, operational outcome, and business measure.
2. Use comparable periods and cohorts; state the source, unit, time window, formula, and assumptions.
3. Label calculated results `scenario estimate` unless the outcome has been measured and verified.
4. Show sensitivity when an assumption materially changes the result.
5. Prefer closing value-evidence gaps and recovering adoption before recommending commercial concessions.
6. Coordinate with `stakeholder-alignment` for executive review, procurement, customer-facing numbers, or commercial decisions.

## Contribute to the response

- Put the concise value narrative and next validation action in `recommended_actions` and rationale.
- Put verified metrics, formulas, assumptions, and sources in `evidence`.
- Put causal overclaims, unsupported savings, and discount-first recommendations in `do_not_do`.
- Lower `confidence` when baseline, measurement period, unit cost, attribution, or customer validation is missing.
- Require human approval for customer-facing ROI numbers, commitments, pricing, discounts, or record changes.
