# Onboarding and Adoption Standard

## Incomplete onboarding
Incomplete onboarding is an early churn signal. Rebuild the success plan around the customer's business outcome, not a generic feature checklist. Define milestones, owners, dates, data dependencies, and measurable adoption outcomes.

## AI deployment squad
When enterprise AI rollout stalls, assign a joint deployment squad from solutions engineering and customer success. The squad should verify tenant readiness, permissioning, security constraints, integration blockers, prompt/workflow design, and end-user enablement. The deliverable is a dated rollout recovery plan with seat-activation targets and named owners.

## Low engagement
When login activity and usage trends fall, first verify whether the customer's operating model changed. Then identify inactive user cohorts, blocked integrations, missing permissions, and training gaps. Use targeted enablement rather than broad communications.

## Success measurement
Track active users, critical workflow completion, time-to-value, support volume, and stakeholder sentiment. Review leading indicators weekly for high-risk accounts and monthly for stable accounts.

## Champion building
If an account has too few internal champions, identify at least one business sponsor and one hands-on power user. Provide them with office hours, targeted use-case assets, and a lightweight success scorecard so adoption momentum does not depend on a single contact.
