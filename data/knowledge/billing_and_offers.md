# Billing and Commercial Guardrails

## Payment failures
Payment failures can cause involuntary churn. Confirm billing contacts, payment methods, procurement requirements, and invoice disputes. Resolve administrative issues before assuming product dissatisfaction.

## Discounts
Discounts should not be the default retention response. A discount is appropriate only when the account has a credible value case, an approved commercial strategy, and a reciprocal commitment such as term extension, expanded adoption, or reference participation.

## ROI scenarios
Offer ROI calculations are scenarios, not causal estimates. State assumptions clearly, separate gross retained revenue from discount cost, and obtain finance approval for material concessions.
