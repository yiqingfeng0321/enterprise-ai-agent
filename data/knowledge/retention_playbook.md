# Customer Retention Playbook

## Support escalation
When a customer has multiple unresolved support tickets, the account team should stabilize the product experience before discussing a commercial concession. Assign an incident owner, document the root cause, publish next-update timing, and confirm closure with the customer. High-value accounts should receive an executive escalation when the issue affects a critical workflow.

## Usage recovery
A material decline in product usage requires workflow-level diagnosis. Compare current activity with the customer's prior baseline, identify the teams and features with the largest drop, and run a focused enablement session. The success plan should include a 14-day adoption target and a named customer owner.

## Renewal readiness
For renewals within 60 days, complete a cross-functional risk review. Present realized value, open risks, adoption gaps, stakeholder alignment, and a dated recovery plan before pricing negotiations. Any discount or contract change requires approval and should be tied to a measurable customer commitment.

## Executive engagement
Executive outreach is useful when the account has high revenue at risk, strategic importance, or repeated operational failures. The message should acknowledge the issue, state the recovery owner, and avoid making unapproved commitments.
